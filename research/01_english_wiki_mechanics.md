## 1. Core loop, calendar and year structure

- You run a game company; create games with attributes **Fun, Creativity, Graphics, Sound**. Many factors affect how popular a game is, how well it sells, how large your fan base is, and whether it can have a sequel. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Time is measured in Months (M) and Weeks (W)**, e.g. events fire at "M5 W2", "M7 W1", "M12 W1". Year 1 starts with 4 staff. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Payroll:** staff are paid annually at the start of **March** (M3); combined salaries deducted from funds. Exception: end of first year — all salaries of year 1 are covered by the "government". If funds are insufficient, the company can dip into a **one-time $150K emergency fund**; if that is insufficient or payroll fails again, consequences can include cancellation of projects. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Global Game Awards (GGA):** annual show every **December** (annual; exact week M12). Entry: a game reached Hall of Fame, i.e. total review score ≥ 32. Once entered once, the company's games always compete against rival companies. Game of the Year chance requires total review score ≥ 36 — even 40 does not guarantee the award. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Gamedex** (E3 parody): annual show at start of **July (M7 W1)**; first Gamedex appears after the first office upgrade. Booths increase company popularity. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Winter holiday sales spike:** "sales tend to spike during the winter holidays, so the timing of a release also affects its success" (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Transcript:Manual_(Game_Dev_Story), page 25); Items page calls it "the crucial **M12W1 sales spike**" (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Game_Dev_Story)).
- **Pumpkin Products** (item salesman) visits annually on **M5 W2**, first time in **Year 2** (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story).
- **Ending:** the game "ends" after **20 years** have passed. Total assets (capital) at that point is saved as your high score. You can continue playing after this. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Transcript:Manual_(Game_Dev_Story) page 27; https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Endgame_(Game_Dev_Story))
- **Console discontinuation notice:** the player is informed **3 months ahead** that a console will be discontinued. (https://kairosoft.fandom.com/wiki/Consoles_(Game_Dev_Story))

### Office upgrades
After every upgrade: +2 more seats, and new Training, Advertising and Hiring methods unlock. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **First upgrade:** upgrade event raised first time after developing one game and obtaining $1,000.0K after year 4 _[Confirm needed on wiki]_. Cost: **$600.0K**.
- **Second upgrade:** event raised after obtaining **$3,500.0K** AND/OR winning 1+ trophies (**Best Design**, **Best Music**) at the Global Game Awards. From **Y4 M12 to Y6 M12 both required**; from **Y7–Y9 either one required**; after **Y10 not required**. Cost: **$2,500.0K**.
- **Contradiction (note):** Achievements page says the "Movin' On Up" achievement = "Accept to move offices for 600K" and "Hittin' the Big Time" = "Accept to move offices for 1,200K" (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements_(Game_Dev_Story)) — i.e. 600K and 1,200K, while the main page states 600K and 2,500K. Both versions recorded; the main-page numbers are probably the modern ones.
- Office 3 is required to **build your own console** (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Consoles_(Game_Dev_Story)).

## 2. Staff / Employees

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Employees_(Game_Dev_Story) (fandom version identical + flavor "Quote" column per employee).

- **44 hire-able employees + 18 outsourced specialists.**
- **Four stats:**
  - **Programming (Program):** general skill; mostly governs speed at which Fun points are produced during development.
  - **Scenario:** quality of game proposals and adding Creativity points during development.
  - **Graphics:** creating Graphics points.
  - **Sound:** composing music / Sound points.
- **Max Power:** each employee has a max Power value; work and training consume Power; Power is restored when staff go home or via Dead Bull (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Training_(Game_Dev_Story)).
- **Leveling up:** costs Research Data; raises all stats AND salary. **Salary compounds ×1.2 per level-up.** Max level 5 per career. Level-ups mainly matter to unlock genres/types and change careers (stat gains are poor value vs training). (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Employees_(Game_Dev_Story))
- **Hardware Engineer unlock:** an employee must be **level 5 in both Director and Producer** to get the Hardware Engineer career — normally implies L5 in all four basic jobs, but employees starting as Director/Producer can circumvent. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Employees_(Game_Dev_Story))
- **Hiring methods:**

| Type | Cost ($) | Unlocked | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Word of Mouth | 50.0K | Initially available | Ask staff to recommend someone |
| Magazine Ad | 120.0K | Initially available | Place an ad in a game magazine |
| Online Ad | 550.0K | Initially available | Search from newbies to veterans |
| Vocational School | 800.0K | Move to office 2 | Look for young kids with promise |
| Open House | 1,800.0K | Move to office 2 | Expensive, but effective |
| Hollywood Agent | 3,500.0K | Move to office 3 | Conduct a worldwide search |

- **Firing:** fired employees retain salary, trained stats and job levels, and can be re-hired via the same method. Used deliberately to drop high-salary Hardware Engineers between console projects. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Employees_(Game_Dev_Story))
- **Moving seats:** employees with smaller Power bars placed closer to the door walk less when leaving for home → small performance increase. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Outsourcing:** outside specialists can be hired per-game for a price (marked "Out"); costs more than employees but give higher boosts / avoid tiring your staff. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Employees_(Game_Dev_Story))

**Full employee tables (44 employees + 18 outsourced, all stats and career levels) are in the Appendix (§A).** Career level columns order: Coder, Writer, Designer, Snd.Eng., Director, Producer, Hard.Eng., Hacker; bold = starting career.

## 3. Careers

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Careers_(Game_Dev_Story)

- **8 careers; each max level 5.** Employees can have levels in all careers, but only one career is active at a time.

| Career | Prerequisites | Focus | Fun | Creativity | Graphics | Sound | Debugging | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coder | — | Debugging | + | + | + | + | ++ | Focuses on Coding stat; needed for Director |
| Writer | — | Proposal | ++ | ++ | + | + | + | Focuses on Scenario stat; needed for Director |
| Designer | — | Alpha | + | ++ | ++ | + | + | Focuses on Graphics; needed for Producer |
| Snd. Eng. | — | Beta | + | ++ | + | ++ | + | Focuses on Sound; needed for Producer |
| Director | L5 Coder AND Writer | Proposal & Debugging | +++ | +++ | + | + | +++ | Needed for Hardware Engineer |
| Producer | L5 Designer AND Snd.Eng. | Alpha & Beta | + | +++ | +++ | +++ | + | Needed for Hardware Engineer |
| Hard. Eng. | L5 Director AND Producer | Console Creation | + | +++ | + | + | +++ | Needed to create new consoles; only Chimpan Z-Force starts with it |
| Hacker | L5 Hard. Eng. | All | +++ | +++ | +++ | +++ | +++ | Very high everything; much higher salary; **cannot** be used to create a console |

- Coder: only adds stat points during development (can't write proposals). Writer: writes game proposals. Designer: designs graphics. Snd. Eng.: composes soundtrack.
- **Hardware Engineer special bonus:** unlocks special console parts by headcount on your team:
  - **Potato Chip** (CPU) — need **4 hardware engineers** on team.
  - **Punch Card** (Media) — need **6 hardware engineers** on team.
  (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Careers_(Game_Dev_Story); prices in Consoles §8)
- **Changing careers:** use a **Career Change Manual** (from the traveling salesman). To move into Director/Producer/Hardware Engineer/Hacker, the employee must be **level 5 in the requisite lower career(s) themselves** — prior experience levels in the target career don't help, nor does another employee's level. Switching back to an advanced career after switching away requires re-leveling all lower careers (big salary hike). (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Careers_(Game_Dev_Story))

## 4. Training

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Training_(Game_Dev_Story)

- Training raises Program/Scenario/Graphics/Sound using **Power and cash** (not Research Data).
- **Diminishing returns:** after enough uses on an employee, a method's effect diminishes possibly to zero; a more powerful method can restore learnability.
- **Super effective training** ("Super Parameter Up"): random chance of **3× the base increase**, even when a training normally gives nothing.
- **Pinball:** +0 or +6 to **each stat**, works up to 999 even after everything else stops; fails 3/4 of the time; no super-effective procs. (main page: "Pinball never stops working (6 to each stat), but it fails to do anything most of the time (3/4)") (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Power exploit:** even if a training method needs more power than the employee has, ≥2 Power points is enough to train → regenerate ~2.5 power, train, repeat.

## 5. Items

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Game_Dev_Story) + https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story

- Sold by **Pumpkin Products** (Salesman), visiting annually **M5 W2 starting Year 2**. **Max 3 items per visit.**
- **Prices of all items rise with each office upgrade.** For every item bought, the cost of the *other* items also goes up — **except Dead Bull and Career Change Manual** (their prices don't rise from buying others; buy as many Career Change Manuals as possible before moving to a new office).
- **Multiple-use items** (require Research Data to use; cost rises with stored RP; effect stays same):
  - **Fun Boost** — +Fun points (best with a Coder)
  - **Creativity Boost** — +Creativity (best with a Writer)
  - **Graphics Boost** — +Graphics (best with a Designer)
  - **Sound Boost** — +Sound (best with a Sound Engineer)
  - **Bug Spray** — removes bugs (faster release; note bug-removal gives RP, so factor that in)
  - **Self-Help Book** — motivates staff; increases points added on next couple of attempts
  - **Diminishing returns:** using multiple boosts on one project: 2nd boost ≈ half effect, 3rd+ may do nothing. Boosts can still be used during debugging (RP from debugging may pay for one last boost).
- **One-time use items** (no RP, bought per use):
  - **Dead Bull** — recharges staff energy + helps gain research data (Red Bull parody)
  - **Career Change Manual** — allows an employee to change jobs.
- **Boost failure:** a failed Boost creates bugs in large numbers (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Creating_games_(Game_Dev_Story)).

## 6. Genres, Types, and Combos

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genres_and_types_(Game_Dev_Story)

- Each game has exactly **one Genre and one Type**. Each genre/type has a **popularity rank (A/B/C)** with consumers and a different production cost.
- **Genres are unlocked** by leveling an employee to a specific level in a specific career (or hiring someone who already has it). **Types are unlocked** by employees at/above a level using specific training methods.
- **Genre/Type level-ups:** "As you develop new games with the same genre or type, you will level up the skill of that genre or type." Before using a specific combination it says **"First Try"**.
- Genre levels also matter for development: **direction points start higher and are increased by 2 when a genre reaches level 2 and level 5** (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Creating_games_(Game_Dev_Story)).
- Full tables: **Genres (20)** in Appendix §B, **Types (79)** in Appendix §C (including job/level/training unlock conditions).

### 6a. Combo grades
- Combination rating ("Combo"): measured from "Not good" to "Amazing!".

| Symbol | Grade | Quote after choosing |
|---|---|---|
| S | Amazing! | "What a great combination!! Your staff is very excited to get to work on this one!" |
| A | Creative. | "That's a bizarre combination... I wonder how this will turn out..." |
| B | Not Bad | "This should be a popular combination. I'm looking forward to seeing it finished." |
| C | Nothing | "At last, it's time to start game development. Good luck!" |
| D | Hmm... | "That's a bit of an odd combination. I'm sure you can pull it off, but..." |
| F | Not Good | "That genre and type don't go together at all... I hope it sells..." |

- **Always-'Nothing' combos (feature or bug, unclear):** genres **Sim RPG (3), Educational (19), Card Game (20)** — any combo involving them is 'Nothing'. Same for types **Harbor (76), Time Travel (77), Spy (78), Celebrity (79)**.

## 7. Game creation (full process)

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Creating_games_(Game_Dev_Story)

### Setup
1. **Platform:** choose a Console Type, CPU and Media for the game (from the console you licensed or built; see §8).
2. **Genre & Type:** one each; cost and consumer popularity vary; combination → Combo grade (above).
3. **Direction** (development mode):
   - **Normal** — initial development cost; no focus.
   - **Speed** — **+20%** to initial cost; sacrifices quality for speed.
   - **Quality** — **+30%** to initial cost; slower dev, more points.
   - **Research** — **+50%** to initial cost; focuses on gaining research data.
   - **Budget+** — **doubles** development cost; combines Speed and Quality to ease staff burden.
4. **Direction points** assigned to 8 attributes of the game:
   **Cuteness, Realism, Approachability, Niche Appeal, Simplicity, Innovation, Game World, Polish.**
   You start with some points; **+2 points when a genre reaches level 2 and level 5**.
5. **Proposal (Writing):** needs an employee with Scenario experience, or outsource it; choice affects game quality (Manual page 6).

### Development flow
- **Writing** starts after setup → **event** if good genre/type match → **Art** starts at **40%** completion → **Music** starts at **80%** completion (high Sound stat recommended) → **Bugs** after the game is finished.
- Staff generate Fun (Program), Creativity (Scenario), Graphics, Sound points; a "prev" (preference) condition means using the same employee repeatedly hurts stats gained (see §11 bugs for the workaround).
- Outsourced specialists marked "Out" can fill roles at a price.

### Release
- After release: name the game, critics review it, then sales start.
- **Critics: each of 4 judges gives up to 10 points; total 40.** Bugs found by critics drop the score. **Total ≥ 32 = Hall of Fame** (also triggers sequel eligibility and GGA entry).
- Announcement of sales-chart position depending on initial sales.
- Sales flow into funds "for the next few months before it wanes and the game is finally taken off the market."
- A rival releasing a same-genre game at the same time affects your sales.

### Sequels
- Eligible if original got ≥ 32/40. Sequel must be started while the original is still in records (i.e. one of the **latest 32 games**). Can be on a different console; **genre and type cannot change**; starts with some points from the original. Each game can be sequeled **once**; a sequel that again scores ≥32 can itself have a sequel. (Also on main page: "if a sequel does not make the Hall of Fame on its own, no more sequels can be made in that franchise.")

### Development events (mid-development)
- **"Make the game better" request (at 25% completion):** cost and success rate depend on the offering employee's stats. **Success:** boost in one category, typically **+25–32 points**. **Failure:** +**20–30 bugs** and decreased hype/fame for the game.
- Passing **50** Fun/sound/etc. → message "game has a lot of fun/sound/etc."
- Reaching **100** in a stat → "Fans are lining up" event.
- **Blackout:** lose **20–50 points**. (Tip page: "Try to Save before blackouts :)")
- **Equipment failure:** lose points.
- **Similar game released** (rival): reduces sales.
- **Too many games of the same category released:** shrinks fanbase.

## 8. Consoles / Hardware

Source: https://kairosoft.fandom.com/wiki/Consoles_(Game_Dev_Story) (fandom version has the full table + Analysis quotes; wiki.gg has the same table without "Length On Market" column) and https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Consoles_(Game_Dev_Story)

- To develop for a console you must **buy a license** (except PC Game), unless you make your own console. More expensive/newer console → more money per game.
- **Discontinuation:** once removed from market, no new games for it. Making a new console removes your old one. **PC Game is never removed**, and **no console released after Mini Status is ever removed**. Player informed **3 months** ahead of discontinuation.
- **Console companies:** Intendro (Nintendo), Senga (Sega), Sonny (Sony), Microx (Microsoft), Nipon (NEC/SNK), Karoisoft (Kairosoft).
- Full console list with prices, unlock dates (Y=year, M=month, W=week), removal dates and market length: **Appendix §E**.

### Making your own console
- Requires ≥1 **Hardware Engineer** on staff and **office level 3**. Takes significant time; fan base usually shrinks at least once while no game is being released (high stats + Dead Bull mitigate).
- **Console Type:** Console **6,000.0K** / Portable **4,000.0K**
- **CPU:** 16-bit Chip **1,000.0K** | 32-bit Chip **10,000.0K** | 64-bit Chip **50,000.0K** | Potato Chip **90,000.0K** (requires 4 hardware engineers)
- **Media:** Cartridge **3,000.0K** | CD-ROM **5,000.0K** | DVD-ROM **15,000.0K** | BD-ROM **35,000.0K** | Punch Cards **93,999.9K** (requires 6 hardware engineers)
- **Ultimate console = Potato Chip + Punch Cards ≈ $190M total** (Tips page: "You need $190-million to build the ultimate console... requires 6 Hardware Engineers").
- **Jupiter 512** (Karoisoft): unlock = 2nd "Game of the Year" award + 16 million sales of one game; dev 350.0K, license 64,000.0K.
- **GameJohn** (Karoisoft): unlock = 5th "Game of the Year" award + 20 million sales of one game; dev 350.0K, license 64,000.0K.

## 10. Research Data (RP)

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story

- Gained by employees **while working and while idle**, from **contract completion** (amount varies with difficulty), and from **debugging (1–3 RP per bug removed)**.
- Uses: **leveling employees** (up to L5, progressively more RP per level) and **Boosts**. Boost RP cost depends on how many boosts already used (more boosts → more RP). **Employee-requested boosts** let you spend more RP to raise success chance **up to 80%**; the RP needed for a given success % depends on the employee's skills (more skills → less RP).
- Endgame farming: repeatedly complete high-requirement contracts for bulk RP.

## 11. Sales, Fans, Hype

- **Fans are divided into age groups** and affect game sales; **fans age over time**, so you must appeal to new young gamers (Manual page 21). Gender also matters (fan letters per age group/gender).
- **Fan letters:** positive-score published games trigger letters that increase popularity in the letter's age group and gender; negative scores trigger the opposite (decrease). (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- **Hype** is built by advertising during development; high enough buzz → "Generate Some Buzz" achievement.
- **Sales spike during winter holidays** (M12 W1 crucial). Sales charts announcement based on initial sales. Sales wane over a few months, then the game is taken off the market.
- Same-genre rival game at same time → reduced sales.
- Fans/hype/game popularity carry modifiers: genre/type popularity with consumers (A/B/C), combo grade, review scores, awards, Gamedex booths, console market share, fans.
- Exact sales formula: **not published on either wiki** (only qualitative rules above).

## 12. Reviews, Awards, Hall of Fame

### Magazine reviews (Game Guy Magazine etc.)

- 10-point messages (8): "A masterpiece!!", "Amazing! I'm impressed!", "An amazingly large game!", "I bought 3 copies myself.", "It's... so beautiful...!", "Perfect!", "The game of the century!", "This'll go down in history!"
- 9-point (10), 8-point (7), 7-point (9), 6-point (6), 5-point (7), 4-point (6), 3-point (6).
- **4 judges × 10 pts = 40 max. 32+ = Hall of Fame.** Different judges can give different scores based on audience appeal ("playability... how it appeals to different audiences").
- Reviews happen after release ("it will be reviewed by critics, and then copies will start to be sold").

### Global Game Awards
- Annual, December. Entry = Hall of Fame (score ≥32). Categories/trophies: **Best Design, Best Music, Worst Game Award, Runner Up, Grand Prize (Game of the Year)**.
- **GOTY criteria (fandom main page adds):** 1) total review score ≥36 (40 not guaranteed); 2) "Getting a score at least of 160 in every category of the game (Fun, Creativity, Graphics, Sound) you sure get the award. However I got it with a score higher than 155 in one category, AND at least 200 in other" — 160 in all four is the sure way. (https://kairosoft.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- Tips: boost all four elements well over 150 each (200 preferred); PC game is enough; sequels make GOTY easy in the last years; 3–4 Hall of Fame games per year thins competition. (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tips_(Game_Dev_Story))
- Hall of Fame entry also = sequel eligibility.

## 13. Contracts

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story

- Available from the same menu as new projects, from the start of the game. Useful early (money + research points); mid/late game not worth it.
- All contracts share mechanics: (1) **payment $100K–$1,000K+** on completion (larger as company expands); (2) **deadline usually 6–13 weeks**; (3) required categories from {Fun, Originality, Graphics, Sound} — at least one, usually two, in varying amounts.
- Harder contracts pay more but need higher category levels in less time. **Failure: lose the fee + lose reputation.**
- RP reward varies with difficulty; **Tool Development** cited as the best RP-per-work contract (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tips_(Game_Dev_Story)).

## 14. Events

## 17. Bugs & Cheats (replicated without cheat tools)

Source: https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bugs_and_cheats_(Game_Dev_Story)

- **"prev" removal:** when the scenario-writing screen appears during game creation, press back → the "prev" condition is removed from the employee → same employee can be used again and again.
- **[Patched]** Genre/type fast leveling by re-entering setup and pressing back; for newer versions doesn't take effect until development starts.
- **[Patched]** Contract (hiring) fees only applied when filling an empty slot; replacing a cheap hire with an expensive one skipped the fee.
- **Frozen employee bug:** fire an employee exactly while he's giving Research Data to another (requires high reflexes) → recipient freezes; cure: have another employee give Research Data to the frozen one.
- **Rehire-after-fire trick:** fire someone (e.g. Hardware Engineer), then re-hire via the same method; if you luckily get them, you pay their original contract fee (e.g. John Gameson $50 Word of Mouth) and get them with the same job.
- **Time Traveller cheat** (manual save/load around boosts, hires, purchases) — described on the Tips page (https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tips_(Game_Dev_Story)).
