Special Events

By IGN-GameGuides, IGN-Cheats, UFSGB
Updated: Mar 27, 2012 7:09pm UTC

Game Dev Story Special Events

  • Game Basics
  • Hiring & Training
  • Hall of Fame
  • Special Events
  • Amazing Games

On occasion, events will occur during the development or release of your game that can affect sales or the quality of your game. There are also events that do not directly affect your game or company, but are important for you to know.

Positive Events

Magazine writes about your game

Fan letters that love your game

Negative Events

Fan letters that hate your game

Finding out your company is releasing something that is too similar to previous games (during development)

Fans of your game are aging (loss of fans because oldest group stops playing and newest grown doesn't much know about you.)

Blackout (you lose half your points on all your game parameters, and no, you can't do anything about it.)

Other Events

This will give you a new option to develop for. The newest console is usually the most expensive option to get a contract to develop for.

During the development of a game or contract, one of your employees may come up and say that they would like to help boost a game parameter. You will have to pay them for doing this, and there is some risk involved, but the reward is worth it.

The risk is that each staff member will have a different percentage chance of succeeding, depending on their statistics. You can use research data to improve the odd, but the succession rate doesn't go any hirer than 80%. If your staffer succeeds, you will be rewarded with a boost in that game parameter, ranging from 30-50 points. If the staffer fails, then you will inherit a slew of bugs.

Gamedex is an in-game gaming convention. With the variety of booth types available to you, it allows you to make a push for interest in your games while you have them on the market or while they are in development. Spending more money will usually yield more visitors, but you will not be penalized for not attending.

The awards ceremony happens every year and any game you have released up to that point in the year.

Sound Design
Awarded $100,000

Art Design
Awarded $100,000

Runner Up
Awarded $300,000

Worst Game
Deducts money from your bank

Game of the Year
Awarded $1,000,000

...And there you have it. Good luck and don't forget that the game doesn't end after 20 years; you can continue on if you so choose.
