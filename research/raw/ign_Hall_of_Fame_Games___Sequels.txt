Once you have gotten the hang of developing your games, and you have a solid development staff, you may be able to create a game with a total review score of 36 or higher.

How to Make a Hall of Fame Game

1

Focus on Fun

 Using the fun boost can earn you anywhere from 1-25 points to your game's fun. (be aware, if you use the same boost more than once on the same game, the amount of bonus points earned per use will continue to decrease).

2

Use your boosts

 If Fun is high, but Sound is lacking, make sure to use the sound boost to help bring your overall game to greatness. (If you're

3

Build on a strong base

 Don't try to get your first Hall of Fame game buy making the oddest pairing of game genre and type. If your team shows excitement for a good combination (see list of awesome combinations), take that as the green light to go for broke on that title.

If you are lucky enough to make a Hall of Fame game, then you have unlocked the ability to make a sequel of that game. You will see the option in the develop menu. Remember not to start on a sequel too soon after releasing the original. You may turn your fans off, they will lose interest in your game and your sales will suffer.
