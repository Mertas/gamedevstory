{{Game Dev Story}}
{{tocright}}
'''Genres and types''' are an important aspect when [[Creating games (Game Dev Story)|making a game]], as each game can only have one of each, and which ones you choose can greatly affect sales. Genres and types all have unique "popularity" with consumers, and the cost to produce a game in any genre or type varies. The key to using genre and types effectively is to [[#Combinations|combine]] complimentary subjects to create combos. Each combo receives a basic rating, ranging from "Not good" to "Amazing!", to measure how  successful your combo is. This plays a key role in making a top-selling game.

'''Genres''' are unlocked by leveling up [[Employees (Game Dev Story)|employees]] to a specific level or higher in a specific [[Careers (Game Dev Story)|career]]. You can also hire someone who already has obtained this level to unlock it. Unlocking '''types''' requires employees at or above a certain level to use specific [[Training (Game Dev Story)|training methods]].
